# Dhairya Bansal — Portfolio

A personal portfolio website showcasing my work across **FinTech, quantitative finance, software systems, blockchain analytics, and AI-powered applications**.

The portfolio is designed as more than a traditional resume website. It presents the systems and projects I am building while documenting the areas I am actively exploring across finance and technology.

## Live Website

🌐 **Portfolio:** https://dhairya-portfolio-one.vercel.app/

## About

I am currently pursuing a **BBA in FinTech alongside ACCA** while independently building projects across quantitative finance, financial analysis, software engineering, blockchain, and AI systems.

My approach to learning is primarily project-driven.

Rather than treating finance, mathematics, and software engineering as separate disciplines, I focus on building systems at their intersection.
